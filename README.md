# MurderMystery

CSPC 225 Final Project

By Esra Petty, Payton Phillips, and Gabrielle de Leeuw

Completed Fall 2026

Welcome to the world of THE MURDER OF CHARLES HUDSON! You are a detective riding on the new fastest rail train in 1872. Everything is perfect- at least, until someone is murdered. Your goal is to solve the murder before you become the next target!
